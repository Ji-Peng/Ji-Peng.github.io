I am currently a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Prof. Jiaheng Zhang. I received my Ph.D. in 2025 under the supervision of Prof. Çetin Kaya Koç at Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA), China, where I also obtained my B.Sc. in 2020. My research primarily focuses on cryptographic engineering, zero-knowledge proofs (ZKP), post-quantum cryptography (PQC), elliptic curve cryptography (ECC), and hardware-software co-design across modern CPUs (AVX-512, RVV, NEON), GPUs, and custom hardware accelerators.

Interests: Cryptographic Engineering Zero-Knowledge Proofs Post-Quantum Cryptography

Industrial Research Projects

Curve25519 Optimization University & Enterprise Project

@Shield Lab & CBG, Huawei
2020.08 - 2020.12

Role: University & Enterprise Collaboration Researcher

Reduced the ROM occupancy of Curve25519 (X25519+Ed25519) from 70KB+ (OpenSSL) to less than 15KB (and under 10KB on target devices) for resource-constrained IoT devices (Cortex-M3 and RISC-V).

SM2 Optimization Internship Project

@CDG, Tencent
2021.07 - 2021.09

Role: Intern (2021 Rhino-Bird Research Elite Training Program) · Shenzhen, China

Optimized the performance of SM2 (Chinese ECC Commercial Standard) on ARMv8-A Linux servers. Compared to OpenSSL, the performance of sign/verify was improved by 8.7X/3.5X on HUAWEI Cloud Kunpeng 920 platform.
